Frequently asked questions

Does AI post entries or close the period for me?

No. It reads, explains and prepares drafts. Approval, posting and closing stay with a user who has the permission, and every suggestion is logged.

Does it support e-invoicing?

Yes. Phase 1 (generation and storage): gapless numbering, the authority's QR format, no deleting an approved invoice, and cancellation by credit note. Phase 2 (integration): we connect your company to the Fatoora platform before its mandated wave date.

Does my company get its own address?

Yes. You choose your company's address when you subscribe, and each company has its own isolated users and data.

How do I move from my current system?

We import master data and balances and reconcile them with you before approval. Enterprise onboarding is run by our team.

Can I delete a wrong invoice or entry?

Not after approval. An invoice is cancelled by a linked credit note and an entry by a reversing entry, so every correction stays visible to your auditor.

How we use your data

We keep your request only to contact you about it. We never sell it or share it with marketers, and you can ask us to delete it by replying to any of our messages. The interactive demo collects nothing, and we use no advertising trackers.
