# Decision: Publish the Tamo head on the marketing site

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Status:** Final (approved by the founder)
- **Supersedes:** the line in [2026-11-18-assistant-name-tamo.md](2026-11-18-assistant-name-tamo.md) that kept the assistant visual private and unreleased.
- **Replaces:** the geometric indigo orb that stood in for Tamo on the public pages.

## Decision
- The founder approved publishing the Tamo head on the Tamnex marketing site.
- The head is the glossy silver sphere with a horizontal light for an eye. It turns and scans in cyan, then settles on a green eye with a check mark.
- Tamo remains an assistant mark under the Tamnex brand. It is not a separate logo, and the Tamnex wordmark is unchanged.
- On the site the mark is a silent loop: WebM (VP9, transparent) with an H.264 MP4 fallback. The settled green-check frame is the poster, and it is the only image shown when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

## Applied in this repository
- Assets: `site/brand/tamo-head.webm`, `site/brand/tamo-head.mp4`, `site/brand/tamo-head.webp`.
- The mark replaces the orb in the hero badge, the close-dashboard capsule, the `#tamo` heading, and the Tamo scenes.
