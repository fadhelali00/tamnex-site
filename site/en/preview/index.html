Frequently asked questions

Does the AI post or close the period for me?

No. It reads, analyses and prepares drafts; approval and close stay with you or whoever you delegate, and every suggestion is logged.

Can I see cash and margins in real time?

Yes. Every invoice, receipt and collection updates the cash and margin indicators immediately, without waiting for month end.

Does it support e-invoicing?

Yes. Phase 1 (generation and storage) is built into every invoice, and we activate your Fatoora integration (Phase 2) before your wave deadline.

How do I move from my current system?

We import master data, opening balances, open invoices and inventory, and reconcile them with you before you approve.

Does my company get its own address?

Yes. You choose your company subdomain at sign-up, and your team signs in from it directly.

Book a live demo

How we use your data

We keep your request only to contact you about it. We never sell it or share it with marketers, and you can ask us to delete it by replying to any of our messages. The interactive demo collects nothing, and we use no advertising trackers.
