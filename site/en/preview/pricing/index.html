Frequently asked questions

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are shown before 15% VAT, with the VAT-inclusive amount under each price.

How does annual billing work?

The full year is paid in advance; its monthly equivalent is shown next to it.

What does “After launch” mean?

The feature is part of the plan and becomes available to the plan's subscribers when it launches, with no change to the agreed price.

What happens if I choose a plan now?

We receive your request and contact you before launch to confirm price and features. No payment is taken before activation.
